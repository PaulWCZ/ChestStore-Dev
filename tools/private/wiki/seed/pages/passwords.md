We keep every shared password in the team's password manager, never in a spreadsheet or a message.

1. Accept the invitation Tom sends you on your first day.
2. Choose a long master password: four random words work well.
3. Turn on two-step sign-in with your phone.

Lost your master password? Tom can reset your access, but not recover what was only in your personal vault.
