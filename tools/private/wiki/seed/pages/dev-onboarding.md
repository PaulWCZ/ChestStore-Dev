Welcome to the tech team. Your first week is about learning how our fixtures are controlled, not shipping.

## Day one

- Get your accounts: see [[it-setup]].
- Clone the controller firmware and the configuration tool.
- Pair with Léa on a small fix.

## How we ship

Every change is reviewed by one other person. We ship on Tuesdays and Thursdays, never on Fridays. See [[deploying]].

```
make test && make firmware
```
