How we win and keep clients. Short on purpose: read it before your first client meeting.

## Who we sell to

Shop chains, hotel groups and museums who care how a space **feels**. Not the cheapest lighting — the lighting that makes people stay.

## The five steps

1. **First call** — understand the place and the deadline. Book a visit.
2. **Visit** — photos, measurements, the light at different hours.
3. **Proposal** — a mood board and a first price range within a week. See [[pricing]].
4. **Quote** — detailed, valid 30 days, signed by Inès.
5. **Handover** — the project goes to the design team, with the visit notes.

> [!NOTE]
> Every client gets a reply within one working day, even if it is only “we are on it”.
