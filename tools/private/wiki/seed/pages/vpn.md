The VPN lets you reach the file server, the printers and the test wall from home or from a client’s site. You need it only for those; email, the Chest and the calendar work without it.

## Connect

1. Open **WireGuard** (installed on every laptop).
2. Turn on the tunnel called **Lumen**.
3. Check that the file server opens. If it does not, turn the tunnel off and on again.

## When it does not work

- On a hotel or train Wi-Fi, sign in to the Wi-Fi’s page first, then turn on the VPN.
- A new laptop needs its own key: ask Tom, it takes five minutes.
- Never share your VPN key or install it on a personal computer.
