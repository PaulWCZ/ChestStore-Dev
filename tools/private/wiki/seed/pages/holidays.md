Everyone gets **25 working days** of paid holiday a year, plus public holidays and **2 extra days** between Christmas and New Year, when the workshop closes.

## How to ask

1. Ask your manager at least **two weeks** ahead — a month for more than a week off.
2. Once they agree, add it to the team calendar so that everyone knows.
3. Before you leave, set an out-of-office reply that names who covers for you.

> [!NOTE]
> Holidays run from 1 June to 31 May. Days not taken by 31 May are lost, unless your manager agreed in writing to carry them over.

## Other leave

| Kind | Days | Tell us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your wedding or civil partnership | 4 | 1 month ahead |
| Birth or adoption (second parent) | 3 + 25 | as soon as you can |
| Moving house | 1 | 1 week ahead |
| Death of a close relative | 3 | as soon as you can |

## Sick days

Tell your manager **before 10:00**, and send the doctor’s note to Sofia within 48 hours. Get well: nobody expects you to answer emails.
