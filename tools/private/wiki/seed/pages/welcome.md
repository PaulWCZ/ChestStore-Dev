We design and build lighting for shops, hotels and museums, from our workshop in Lyon. There are 34 of us: designers, engineers, a sales team and an office team who keep everything running.

This handbook is where we write down how things work, so that nobody has to ask twice. If something here is wrong or missing, fix it — every editor can.

## How we work

- **We say what we will do, then do it.** A promise to a client is a promise from all of us.
- **We write things down.** Decisions go in the wiki, not in someone’s head.
- **We ask early.** A question on Monday saves a mistake on Friday.
