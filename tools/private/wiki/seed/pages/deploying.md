Updates go to client sites in three waves, so that a mistake reaches few fixtures.

1. **Workshop** — the test wall in the workshop, for one day.
2. **Pilot sites** — our office and two friendly clients, for three days.
3. **Everyone** — the rest, over a week, at night.

> [!WARNING]
> Never update a museum during opening hours, even if the change looks harmless.

If anything goes wrong, follow [[incident]].
