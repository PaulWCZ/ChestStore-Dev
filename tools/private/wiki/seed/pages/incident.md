A site is dark, or a controller does not answer. Stay calm and follow the list.

- [ ] Call the client back within 30 minutes, even without news
- [ ] Check the site’s controller from the configuration tool
- [ ] Roll back the last update if it went out in the last 48 hours
- [ ] Write what happened in the incident log, with the times
- [ ] Tell Camille if the client’s opening hours are affected

After it is fixed, we meet for 20 minutes to understand why — never to blame.
